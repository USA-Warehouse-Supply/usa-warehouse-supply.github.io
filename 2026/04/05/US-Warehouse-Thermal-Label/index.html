Thermal paper prints shipping labels, barcodes, pick lists without ink. Critical for US warehouse speed and compliance.

1. Types

Type Durability Cost Best For
Direct thermal Fades in months Low Short-term (shipping labels, pick lists)
Thermal transfer Lasts 3+ years Higher + ribbon Long-term (inventory, asset tags)

Grades:

  • Grade B (low volume, <500 orders/day): fades 6–12 mo
  • Grade A (mid volume, 1k–5k orders/day): lasts 1–2 years
  • Premium (5k+ orders/day): lasts 3+ years, extreme conditions

Look for water-resistant top-coat and strong acrylic adhesive.

2. Key Specs

  • Size: 4x6″ (standard shipping – UPS/FedEx/USPS), 3x2″ (small tags), 4x8″ (pallets)
  • Core: 1″ (desktop printers) or 3″ (industrial printers)
  • Grammage: 80–90 g/m² (standard), 100+ g/m² (heavy-duty)

3. Environmental Factors

  • Heat: direct thermal fades quickly >90°F → use thermal transfer or premium coated
  • Cold: below 32°F makes paper brittle → store at 50–75°F
  • Humidity: water-resistant top-coat & sealed storage
  • UV light: direct thermal fades in days → thermal transfer or UV-protected paper

4. Compliance & Compatibility

Carrier requirements:

  • UPS/FedEx: 4x6″, 203 dpi (FedEx Express needs 300 dpi)
  • USPS: 4x6″ direct thermal works, thermal transfer for international

BPA-free: Required in CA, NY, WA. Choose BPA-free paper for regulated states.

5. Cost & Suppliers

  • Buy in bulk (10+ rolls = 10–15% off)
  • Larger rolls (500+ labels) reduce changeovers
  • Shelf life: 1–2 years – don’t overstock

6. Scenario Comparison

Warehouse Size Recommended Paper
Small e‑commerce (500–1k orders/day) Grade B direct thermal, 4x6″, 1″ core, 80 g/m², BPA‑free
Large fulfillment (5k+ orders/day) Premium thermal transfer, 4x6″, 3″ core, 100 g/m², BPA‑free
Feature Direct Thermal Thermal Transfer
Print longevity 6–12 months 3+ years
Cost per label $0.01–0.02 $0.03–0.05 + ribbon
Best for Short-term labels Long-term labels
Environmental resistance Low High

7. Storage & Maintenance

Storage:

  • 50–75°F, 40–60% humidity, sealed containers
  • Avoid stacking heavy objects, use FIFO

Printer maintenance:

  • Clean print head every 5–10 rolls (isopropyl alcohol)
  • Calibrate after paper change
  • Adjust pressure; replace worn heads/rollers every 1–2 years

8. Recommended Configurations

Daily Orders Budget Paper Type Specs
0–500 Low Grade B direct (BPA‑free) 4x6″, 1″ core, 80 g/m², 250 labels/roll
500–5k Mid Grade A direct (BPA‑free) 4x6″, 3″ core, 80 g/m², 500 labels/roll
5k+ High Premium thermal transfer (BPA‑free) 4x6″, 3″ core, 100 g/m², 1k labels/roll
Any Eco BPA‑free premium direct 4x6″, 1/3″ core, 80–100 g/m²

Final Thoughts

Balance cost, durability, and carrier compatibility. Store properly, maintain printers. Right thermal paper keeps labels scannable and operations smooth.
